The Islands

Three islands, one day. Here is what waits on each of them.

01

Mahé

Where every day begins

Every excursion starts and ends at the Marine Charter Jetty on Mahé. From here the boat heads out across the water towards Praslin and La Digue.

  • Marine Charter Jetty
  • Departures from 07:00
  • Back on Mahé by 16:50
See the excursions

02

Praslin

Coco de Mer and Anse Lazio

After a scenic boat ride you arrive on Praslin for a guided bus tour to Glacis Noire, home of the Coco de Mer, followed by time on the beautiful Anse Lazio beach.

  • 1h 15m scenic boat ride
  • Glacis Noire and the Coco de Mer
  • 1h 45m at Anse Lazio
View the full timeline

03

La Digue

Bikes, beaches and a buffet lunch

A short boat transfer brings you to La Digue, where lunch is a buffet at Fish Trap Restaurant. Explore by bicycle or club car to the famous Anse Source d’Argent and, on the full-day trip, take a guided tortoise and vanilla plantation tour.

  • Buffet lunch at Fish Trap
  • Bicycle or club car
  • Anse Source d’Argent

Anse Source d’Argent entrance fee: €13 or SCR 150 per person, payable on site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your island day.

Where do the tours depart from?

All excursions depart from the Marine Charter Jetty, Mahé, and return there in the afternoon.

What does the price include?

Tours are €130 per person plus SCR 25 port tax. Inclusions vary by package: see the excursion pages for the full checklist.

Is there any extra fee on the day?

Yes. The Anse Source d’Argent entrance fee on La Digue is €13 or SCR 150 per person, payable on site.

Is lunch included?

The Full-Day Island Excursion includes a buffet lunch at Fish Trap Restaurant on La Digue. The La Digue Day Express is self-guided and does not list lunch as included.

Are bicycles included?

Yes, bicycle rental is included in both packages.

How do I book?

Fill in the booking form to send your request on WhatsApp, or message, call or email us. Our team confirms your date, usually within an hour, then emails you a secure payment link. Your booking is confirmed once you have paid.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, sunscreen, a hat, a towel, water and a camera are a good start for a day on the islands.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation is free up to 12 hours before departure.

How much do children pay?

Children aged 0–2 travel free of charge, ages 3–10 pay half price, and ages 11 and up pay the full price.

How and when do I pay?

Full payment is taken upon booking. After we confirm your date you will receive an email with a secure payment link, and you pay through that link.

Which days do you run, and what about the weather?

Trips run daily, weather permitting.

Which languages do you speak?

We can help you in English, French and Creole.

When can I contact you?

We take enquiries every day from 7:00 to 22:00 and usually reply within an hour.

Can I book a private charter?

Yes. Tell us your date, group size and plans and we will give you a price.

I didn’t get the payment email. What should I do?

Check your spam or junk folder first. If it isn’t there, message us on WhatsApp and we will send it again.
